How to read these results

Ratings range from 1 to 5. Question averages use valid answers only. Support and trip scores are calculated for respondents who answered every question in that category, then averaged with equal weight per respondent. Low ratings count individual answers, not volunteers.

Only trips with at least five responses are published. A question or category also needs five valid answers. Filters and trends use only published values, so some answers may be excluded. Dates refer to the trip, not submission time. The latest 30/90 days are measured from the update date.

Pre-trip questions cover travel coordination and preparation by Education, Logistics, IM and SM. Trip questions cover accompaniment, accommodation, meals and flight arrangements. Names, comments and submission identifiers are excluded. This website is publicly accessible and small-group suppression does not guarantee anonymity.
